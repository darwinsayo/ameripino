Two kitchens, one long table.

Lutong bahay is Tagalog for home-cooked, and it's the only way we know how to do it. Order a plate from each side of the board and pass everything around.

Filipino favorites the lola side

  • LumpiaCrisp fried rolls, served hot with dipping sauce.
  • SinigangSour tamarind soup, warm and savory.
  • AdoboBraised in soy sauce and vinegar, served over rice.
  • PancitStir-fried noodles with vegetables and meat.

American classics the backyard side

  • Pulled porkSlow-cooked, tender, and piled high.
  • BBQ ribsSmoky, saucy, and fall-off-the-bone.
  • Garlic friesGolden fries tossed in plenty of garlic.

Save room. Then ask what's special.

Two sweet ways to cool down after the ribs, and one dish that changes every single day.

Halo-halo

Shaved ice layered with sweet toppings. Mix it all up before the first spoonful; that's what the name means.

Boba

Chewy tapioca pearls in a cold, creamy drink. The wide straw is not optional.
